## Slice 1 — Menu cleanup and audio controls (small, low risk)

- `render.rs` + `main.rs`: `draw_slider(x, y, w, h, label, value, selected, u)` widget; mouse
  drag and Left/Right; use for MUSIC and EFFECTS volume.
- Menu layout: three groups with headings — **PLAY** (Start run, Play files/folder, Live audio,
  LAN Rally), **MUSIC** (library, track prev/next), **OPTIONS** (difficulty, volumes, sync
  offset, visuals, fullscreen) behind a sub-screen so the first screen is short.
- Jukebox: when a menu track ends (stop looping; `finished`) or after ~90 s of preview, advance

- Verify: `heartline.exe --mute --selftest tmp\m --wall --shots 6 --secs 7`, read the PNG.

## Slice 2 — Difficulty: harder, more tiers, themed names
Player: too easy even on Hard, though closer to right; wants more tiers, named on a theme with
a one-line description (their example: hardcore = "FLATLINE").
- Replace `DIFFS` (`main.rs:303`) with a table of `{ name, blurb, mult, no_fail, hp, ramp }`.
  Proposed heart-rate theme (multiplier on the per-second budgets in `level.rs`):
  | Name | Blurb | mult | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | RESTING | "No-fail. Sparse attacks, long warnings." | 0.7 | current Chill |
  | STEADY | "The intended first run." | 1.15 | a bit above old Normal |
  | ELEVATED | "Denser patterns, shorter warnings." | 1.6 | above old Hard |
  | TACHYCARDIA | "Two attacks overlap. Enemies fire more." | 2.1 | new |
  | FLATLINE | "One life. No mercy window. Full pressure from the first bar." | 2.6 | 1 HP-loss = big penalty or 3 HP, `MERCY_DROP` off, `RAMP` starts at 0.8 |
- `game.rs`: make `RAMP`, `MERCY_DROP`, `HIT_INVULN`, starting HP and the `P_*` unlock
  thresholds per-difficulty instead of constants. `level.rs`: shorten `pulse_lead` and allow
  overlapping tile + laser above ELEVATED (today only `diff > 1.2`).
- Re-baseline with `--sim "E:\Libraries\Music\Straftat" --sample 12 --bot-delay 120`.
  Old numbers: Chill 1.1 / Normal 1.6 / Hard 2.7 hits per minute. The player found Hard too
  easy, so target roughly STEADY ≈ 2.5, ELEVATED ≈ 4, TACHYCARDIA ≈ 6, FLATLINE ≈ 8 and adjust
  from their next report (the bot at 120 ms clearly under-rates this player).

## Slice 3 — The gun: tighter to the song, constant DPS
Player: "my character's attacks should sync to the song way better, though DPS should be
consistent between songs, so damage needs to dynamically adjust to rate of fire."
- Today `game.rs::update_player` picks one beat subdivision nearest a target interval
  (`FIRE_INTERVALS`) and scales damage by a clamped factor (0.6–1.7), so DPS is only roughly
  constant and the gun ignores the actual drums.
- Change: fire on the song's real rhythm — a per-song "gun track" built in `level.rs` from
  onsets (hats/snares snapped to the 16th grid, grid fill where sparse), density chosen per
  TEMPO upgrade level. Damage per shot = `target_dps × time since previous shot` (unclamped
  within sane bounds), so sparse bars hit harder and DPS is exactly the same on every song.
  Bigger shots look and sound bigger (size, muzzle, a soft tick SFX on the beat).
- Live mode keeps the subdivision method, with the same exact damage formula.
- Test: unit test that integrated damage over 60 s is within 2 % across 70 / 110 / 170 BPM.

## Slice 4 — The Heart, intra-song progression, and purgatory (the big design piece)
Player: shooting a sitting Heart as the only objective is boring; after the kill the rest of
the song is empty; purgatory (their name for the post-kill state — use it everywhere) is "kind
of nice but weird" and needs planning; intra-song progression needs planning and integration.
Proposal (needs the player's OK on direction before building):
- **Phases tied to the song's sections, not one HP bar.** Each section is a phase with its own
  Heart form and objective; the HUD overview bar shows them. The Heart cannot be finished
  before the final section, so the song never goes empty.
- **The Heart moves and changes.** Forms per section kind: *Calm* — shell closed, it roams
  slowly, only weak points on its orbiting rings can be hit; *Groove* — anchors, opens on the
  downbeat (damage window on the beat); *Surge* — splits into 2–3 linked cores that must be
  broken in order; *Drop* — charges across the arena along a telegraphed lane.
- **Side objectives in a run** for variety: break shield nodes at the arena rim, carry a charge
  from a pickup into the Heart, survive a no-shooting bar, kill the elite to stun the Heart.
- **Purgatory** becomes a designed reward phase, entered only when a phase is beaten early: the
  arena inverts (palette, floor falls away, ridgelines become vertical), everything is
  shootable for score, upgrade choices appear, and it ends when the next section starts — a
  breather with a clock, never the rest of the song.
- Needs: `Heart` gains position, form and weak points (`game.rs`); `level.rs` emits phase
  events; `render.rs` gets forms and the purgatory look. Largest slice: split in three
  (movement + phases, forms, purgatory).

## Slice 8 — LAN Rally
Player: only works with the firewall off; "Crack"/snare is unfair ("how am I supposed to hit 2
balls at once?"); the mode needs love.
- Firewall: the exe has no inbound rule. Options: document/offer a one-click "allow Heartline
  on private networks" (needs admin; the user must approve — never change it silently), or make
  joining outbound-only where possible (host still needs inbound). Check `net.rs` for the
  ports and whether discovery uses broadcast (often blocked on "Public" network profiles).
- Balance in `rally.rs`: never require two simultaneous returns — a second ball spawns only on
  the opposite half of the beat, or snare balls are slower "lobs". Read
  `docs/plans/lan-rally.md` before touching it.

### Slice 10 — Song phases with identity (feeds slices 4 and 9)
Player's example, "The Reef": changes at 0:22, 0:45 (a background layer enters), 1:07, 1:30,
2:15, 3:00, 3:22. Phases should be compared with each other (some repeat, some do not), each
distinct phase gets its own visuals matching its feel, and background chimes / secondary
melodies should have their own visuals and/or enemies.
- `analysis.rs`: per-bar feature vector (band balance, chroma-like 12-bin fold of the
  spectrum, onset density per band, brightness, stereo-less "layer count" proxy = number of
  active spectral bands). Boundaries from novelty (exists); then **cluster sections by
  similarity** (cosine distance, agglomerative, threshold) into phase ids A, B, C... so
  `Section` gains `pub phase: u8` and `pub layers: u8`. Unit test on the synth themes (known
  structure) and check The Reef against the times above.
- **Layer onsets:** detect a new sustained band entering (the 0:45 case) and melodic onsets in
  the upper-mid band that are off the drum grid ("chimes"); emit `Ev::Layer { id, on }` and
  `Ev::Chime { pitch_bin, strength }`.
- Game/visuals: each phase id owns a look (palette family, floor style, rim visualiser, Heart
  form, weather) and an enemy/attack set; a repeated phase brings its look back. Chimes spawn
  a dedicated light enemy ("wisp") or sparkle visuals at pitch-mapped positions.

### Slice 11 — The director ("anger" budget)
Player's idea, adopted: each mechanic / enemy / attack has a hidden meter that fills with
time, position in the song and run depth; it is only spent when the music offers a moment that
suits that mechanic, or when the meter is very high and no better moment is coming soon.
- `level.rs`: replace the separate token buckets with a `Director`: per-mechanic
  `{ charge, rate, cost, fit(moment) }`. Offline we know the whole song, so "is a better
  moment coming soon" is an exact look-ahead (best fit within the next N seconds vs. now).
  Live mode uses the threshold-rises-with-charge rule only.
- Fit examples: lasers ↔ sustained phrases; tile phrases ↔ bar lines with strong downbeats;
  dart streams ↔ dense hats; elites/shockwave ↔ drops; wisps ↔ chimes; Heart form change ↔
  phase boundary.
- Keeps difficulty song-independent (charge rates are per second) while making every use land
  on the best available musical moment.

### Slice 12 — Rewards that fit the song and the performance
- Reward moments are placed by the song (calm phases, phase boundaries, the end), not by XP
  alone. At each one the offer scales with a **performance grade** since the last reward (hits
  taken, accuracy on Heart windows, combo, side objectives): C = 1 common choice, B = 3
  choices, A = 3 with a rare, S = rare + a heal. Show the grade briefly so it reads as earned.
- Variety in delivery: orbs (current), a chest dropped by the elite, a "resonance" pickup that
  must be carried to the Heart, and an end-of-song pick of 1 from 3 run modifiers.

### Slice 13 — Always going deeper (run structure, RoR2-stage feel)
- Each song is a **depth** with its own biome (from slice 9/10 mood), announced on entry
  ("DEPTH 3 — <track>"), with a hand-off sequence: song ends → Heart collapses → the arena
  falls away into the next biome's vista → grade + reward → next track rises through `warp`.
  Run complete / death get their own outro instead of a cut to a results table.
- Depth raises pressure, unlocks enemy and attack tiers, and adds one visible modifier per
  depth so later songs play differently, not just harder.

### Slice 14 — M3U playlists
- Accept `.m3u` / `.m3u8` wherever a file or folder is accepted (drag-drop, PLAY FILES, MUSIC
  LIBRARY, command line): parse lines, skip `#` comments, resolve relative paths against the
  playlist's folder, keep only existing audio files. `music_dir` in `heartline.cfg` may point
  at a playlist file.
